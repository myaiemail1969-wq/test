# Project Aeria — Advertising & Video Script Kit

A working file for building ads, trailers, demo videos, and social posts for
Project Aeria. Pull any section straight into a script doc, storyboard tool,
or ad platform — everything here is written to be copy-pasted and adapted.

## 1. Core Positioning

**One-line pitch**
> A plug-in offline AI advisor, reference library, and encrypted vault — for when the grid, the cloud, and the signal all go down.

**Elevator pitch (≈30 seconds, read aloud)**
> Project Aeria is a USB drive that turns into a complete offline survival and knowledge system. Plug it into any computer — Windows, Mac, or Linux — and you get a local AI advisor trained across medicine, fabrication, and engineering, a searchable library of field manuals and reference texts, and an encrypted vault for your most important documents. No internet. No accounts. No cloud. Just you, your drive, and everything you need to know.

**Taglines (pick by tone)**
- "Knowledge that doesn't need a signal."
- "Plug it in. It's ready."
- "The internet, minus the internet."
- "Your library. Your advisor. Your vault. One drive."
- "Built for the day the network isn't there."
- "Offline by design. Ready by default."

### D. Long-Form Demo (2–3 minutes, YouTube / website)

Use this as a scene outline — narrate live over a real screen recording of the
system booting and answering questions.

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:15)** — "What happens to everything you rely on the
   internet for, the day the internet isn't there?"
2. **The reveal (0:15–0:40)** — Show the drive, the label, the boot sequence
   on Windows. Explain: one plug-in, no install, no account.
3. **Meet the Council (0:40–1:30)** — Live-demo each advisor with a real
   question:
   - @Surgeon: "How do I stabilize a suspected fracture with no splint?"
   - @Blacksmith: "How do I tell if this scrap metal is good steel?"
   - @Engineer: "How do I build a gravity-fed water filter?"
   Show the structured responses on screen (IMMEDIATE THREATS / PRIORITY
   ACTIONS / etc. for the Surgeon; MATERIAL / PROCESS STEPS / HAZARDS for
   the Blacksmith).
4. **The Library (1:30–1:55)** — Show a search across the indexed archive;
   highlight that answers are grounded in real field manuals and texts, not
   invented.
5. **The Vault (1:55–2:20)** — Show encrypting a document, show the .gpg
   file, explain AES-256 and the "no plaintext" policy.
6. **Cross-platform proof (2:20–2:45)** — Unplug, replug into a different OS,
   show identical boot.
7. **Close (2:45–3:00)** — Logo card + tagline + where to learn more.

## 4. Key Selling Points (use across all formats)

Pick 3–4 per ad — don't try to cram all of these into one spot:

- **Fully offline** — no internet connection, cloud account, or external
  service of any kind, ever.
- **Three expert advisors in one system** — @Surgeon (medicine), @Blacksmith
  (metallurgy & fabrication), @Engineer (structural/civil/off-grid systems).
- **Grounded, not invented** — answers are pulled from a real, indexed
  library of field manuals and reference texts, with sources behind them.
- **Encrypted personal vault** — AES-256, zero plaintext, zero cloud.
- **Cross-platform, plug-and-go** — identical experience on Windows, macOS,
  and Linux; nothing to install.
- **Vision support** — can analyze photos locally (identify parts, plants,
  injuries) without any image ever leaving the device.
- **One drive, complete system** — model, library, interface, and vault all
  live in one place.

## 5. Visual & Tone Direction

**Palette:** dark slate background (`#12161C`) with a single warm amber
accent (`#E8A84B`) — evokes a lamp or signal fire in the dark. Avoid bright,
clinical, "tech startup" blues; this should feel grounded, analog, durable.

**Typography mood:** monospace / terminal-style for on-screen UI shots
(reinforces "this really runs locally"); clean sans-serif for marketing text
overlays.

**Pacing:** Calm and deliberate, not frantic. The emotional register is
*quiet confidence and preparedness*, not panic — let silence and the glow of
the screen do the work. Save quick cuts for montage beats only.

**B-roll ideas:**
- Power outage / storm footage (rain on a window, a city skyline going dark)
- Hands plugging in a USB drive, in close-up, slow motion
- Terminal boot sequences with amber text scrolling
- Workshop/forge footage (sparks, hand tools, scrap metal)
- A field dressing being applied, a water filter being assembled
- A drive sitting alongside analog preparedness gear (flashlight, radio,
  first-aid kit, multitool, paper maps)

**Sound design:** Low ambient hum, a single mechanical "click" when the
drive is inserted, soft terminal "type-on" sound for on-screen text, minimal
or no music until the final logo card.

## 6. Audience Angles

Use these to tailor the same core message for different platforms/communities.

**Preparedness / survivalist audiences**
- Lead with: "the day the grid goes down."
- Emphasize: self-reliance, no dependency on infrastructure, the Council as
  a stand-in for experts you can't call.

**Off-grid / homestead / rural audiences**
- Lead with: "no signal where you live? that's exactly who this is for."
- Emphasize: practical day-to-day use (fabrication, water systems, off-grid
  power), not just emergencies.

**Maker / tech / open-source audiences**
- Lead with: "a local LLM, a real archive, AES-256 — all on a $30 drive."
- Emphasize: the technical build (llama.cpp, GGUF models, indexed retrieval,
  cross-platform launchers), transparency, and that it's inspectable.

**General "peace of mind" / gift audiences**
- Lead with: "the thing you hope you never need, but are glad you have."
- Emphasize: simplicity ("plug it in, it's ready"), the vault for family
  documents, and that no technical setup is needed to *use* it day to day.
